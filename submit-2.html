Paste a volunteer posting from an organization’s own page and the AI will draft the details, or fill the form in by hand. Either way, you check each section against the posting, and we review every listing before it goes live.

Privacy: the text you paste is sent to Google’s Gemini AI to read it. Don’t paste personal information. Your account data is never sent to the AI.

The AI can make mistakes, especially with hours, ages and names. Treat what it fills in as a draft to check.
