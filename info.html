About VolunNetBC

VolunNetBC helps BC high school and college students find volunteer roles near them, check the age rules, and apply on the organization’s own page. It also gives students a way to keep a verified record of their hours, because hours count once a supervisor confirms them.

Every listing links to the organization’s own application page. Details can change, so always confirm with the organization before you apply. Listings marked “Sample” are placeholders and not verified.

Privacy and safety

  • Accounts: a username and a password. We don’t ask for your email, real name or birthdate.
  • What we keep: the roles you save, and your sign-off requests: the role, the organization, the hours, the supervisor’s name, the domain of their email (the part after the @), and any reference letter they write. We don’t store supervisors’ email addresses.
  • Who can see it: your saved roles, hours and letters are visible only to you and the site’s administrator. Sharing with partner organizations is off by default, and no partner can see anything yet.
  • Your location: if you use “Near me”, your position is used in your browser to sort roles and is never sent to us.
  • AI: when someone submits a listing, the pasted posting text is sent to Google’s Gemini AI to read it. Don’t paste personal information. Your account data is never sent to the AI.
  • Under 19: in BC, students under 19 are minors. Ask a parent or guardian before sharing your profile with anyone.
  • Hosting: the site runs on Vercel and stores data with Supabase.
  • Problems: use the Report button on any listing, or write to YOUR-EMAIL-HERE to ask for your data to be deleted or a listing to be removed.

This page describes how the site works today. It isn’t legal advice, and it will be updated as the site changes.
